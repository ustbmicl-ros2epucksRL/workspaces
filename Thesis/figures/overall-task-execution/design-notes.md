# Spatial Continuity

The composition communicates through physical space. A restrained building plan establishes scale and movement, while small robot bodies make team organization visible. The viewer should be able to follow the mission without reading a sequence of boxes. Carefully resolved walls, openings, paths, and placements supply the explanatory structure.

Blue describes the journey and amber describes coordinated work at the destination. Quiet gray geometry keeps the environment legible without competing with the moving team. Color is paired with position, path style, and short labels so the information remains understandable without relying on hue alone.

Repeated robot forms indicate temporal continuity. Earlier positions are translucent; the constrained-passage state is more definite; destination activity is enlarged beside the map. The same carefully crafted geometry is reused throughout, with deliberate clearance from walls and consistent visual scale within each view.

Typography is sparse and subordinate to the scene. Three spatial annotations identify departure, organized navigation, and work at the destination. The two enlargements show alternative task types, not simultaneous teams. Meticulous alignment and line hierarchy keep the drawing precise enough for research discussion and vector export.

This is a conceptual task illustration, including proposed navigation and system integration. The environment is an original explanatory layout rather than a reproduction of an implemented simulator map. The blocked branch is static; the robots initially lack its traversability information.
